AR retail product placement

A mobile shopping prototype that lets users select a product and place it on a detected real-world surface.

Experience flow

Front page→Four-product gallery→AR product details→Interaction buttons

The app uses one Unity scene with separate canvases. AR features activate on the product details canvas. A touch raycast finds a tracked plane, instantiates the selected prefab, and hides the plane overlay after placement. Moving back clears the placed object.
